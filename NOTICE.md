Vendored from KiCad 9.0.x. Files retain their upstream names.

## Other 3D model sources

Other 3D models come from third-party sources, such as manufacturer CAD
downloads, Component Search Engine (SamacSys) and EasyEDA / LCSC. They remain
subject to their original providers' terms. Confirm those terms before
redistributing this repository.

## Modifications

A 3D model may be renamed to match its footprint (LIBRARY_CONVENTION.md
section 5). It may also be rotated and translated so it sits correctly on the
footprint origin (section 5.2). Otherwise its geometry is left as downloaded.

Where a vendor model does not match the datasheet exactly, the footprint copper
follows the datasheet, not the model.
